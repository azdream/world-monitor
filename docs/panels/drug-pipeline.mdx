title: "Drug Pipeline"
description: "Late-stage drug trials grouped by estimated regulatory decision window and filterable by drug type, plus recent original FDA approvals."
---

The **Drug Pipeline** panel answers one question: which new drugs could reach a regulatory decision, and roughly when. It lists industry-sponsored Phase 3 trials by the earliest quarter a decision could land, and separately lists recent original FDA approvals.

## What the panel shows

**Pipeline tab** — one row per Phase 3 (or Phase 2/3) trial:

- **Candidate** — the lead investigational drug in the trial.
- **Drug type badge** — small molecule, antibody, ADC, cell therapy, gene therapy, RNA, vaccine, peptide / protein, or other.
- **NEW / EXPANSION badge** — `NEW` when the candidate is not yet an approved drug; `EXPANSION` when the trial tests an already-approved drug in a new use.
- **Estimated decision window** and the trial's primary completion date.

Rows are grouped by the earliest quarter of the estimated window. Two dropdowns filter by drug type and therapeutic area (each option shows its count), and a checkbox hides label expansions. Each row links to the trial on ClinicalTrials.gov.

**Recent approvals tab** — original NDA and BLA approvals from the last 180 days, with drug type, a `NEW` badge for new molecular entities, and a `PRIORITY` badge for priority review.

Panel id is `drug-pipeline`; canonical component is `src/components/DrugPipelinePanel.ts`.

## How the decision window is estimated

The window is a heuristic, not an announced date:

- **Earliest** = primary completion + 10 months (4 months to file, 6-month priority review).
- **Latest** = primary completion + 22 months (10 months to file, 12-month standard review including the filing period).

Regulators do not publish decision goal dates, and sponsors disclose them only in press releases and filings, which this panel does not ingest. A sponsor-announced date always supersedes the estimate. Trials can also fail, be delayed, or never lead to a filing.

## How you reach it

- **Cmd+K**: type *drug pipeline*, *pharma*, or *clinical trials*.
- **Availability by variant**: enabled by default in the **finance** variant; registered but off by default in the **full** variant.

## Data sources

Two RPCs back the panel:

- `GET /api/pharma/v1/list-drug-pipeline` — accepts `modality`, `therapeutic_area`, `novel_only`, and `limit` (default 200, max 500).
- `GET /api/pharma/v1/list-drug-approvals`

The seeder (`scripts/seed-drug-pipeline.mjs`) combines three public sources, none of which requires registration:

- **ClinicalTrials.gov API v2** — industry-led interventional Phase 3 trials whose primary completion date falls between 12 months ago and 30 months ahead. This is the only hard dependency.
- **ChEMBL** — molecule type (drug type) and approval status for name matching. If ChEMBL is unavailable, drug type falls back to name stems and the registry's intervention type.
- **openFDA `drugsfda`** — original NDA/BLA approvals. `OPENFDA_API_KEY` is optional and only raises the daily request quota. If openFDA is unavailable, the approvals tab reports no data rather than an empty list.

Results are written to `pharma:drug-pipeline:v1` in Redis with a 72-hour TTL. The published pipeline is capped at 1,500 trials, soonest first, with a fifth of the cap reserved for label expansions.

## Known limits

- **CBER products are missing from approvals.** Drugs@FDA covers CDER only, so vaccines and cell or gene therapies do not appear in the approvals tab. They do appear in the pipeline tab.
- **Drug type is partly inferred.** Candidates with only a company code name and no ChEMBL entry are typed from the registry's `DRUG` / `BIOLOGICAL` / `GENETIC` label, which cannot tell an antibody from another biologic.
- **US decision timing only.** The review-clock assumptions are FDA's; other regulators differ.

## Refresh cadence

The seeder runs **daily** in the health seed bundle. The key is allowed up to 48 hours in `api/health.js` (`maxStaleMin: 2880`) before the health surface escalates.
